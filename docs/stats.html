About Player Stats

Regular-season stats for the selected organizations’ current players, including stats earned with previous teams.

Statistics dates will appear when player data loads.

Tabs

Batters and Pitchers show the complete lists. IF and OF use primary MLB position; SP and RP use stats in the selected range:

  • IF: C, 1B, 2B, 3B, SS. OF: LF, CF, RF, OF.
  • SP: 2+ starts. RP: 2+ saves or fewer than 2 starts. Players with 2+ starts and 2+ saves appear in both, regardless of position label.
  • Primary DHs and players without a listed position are excluded from IF and OF. Ohtani is displayed as DH for batting and SP for pitching.

Eligibility

Owner Status uses the same test rosters as Standings. Ownership applies across statistics ranges; filters only include players in this page’s postseason player pool.

Red IL badges show injured-list status as of the range's roster date, not an expected return date.

Minimum AB applies to all batting views; minimum IP applies to all pitching views, regardless of sorting. Minimums use the selected range; zero includes everyone. Optioned and injured-list players are included; free agents and released players are excluded.

Last 30 days ends on the published through-date, includes that day, and stays within the season year.

Sorting

Default: most HR for batters, most K for pitchers. Select a column heading to sort; select again to reverse.
